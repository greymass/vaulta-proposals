## Purpose

This rate card sets **Manager and Technical Reviewer fees and responsibilities** under **Framework 12.1**. It must be published before either role is contracted. The **Framework (Exhibit A)** defines their authority, and the **contracts in Exhibit F** incorporate this rate card.

---

## Payment rules

Managers and Reviewers are paid for **assessing work**. The assessment fee is the same whether they **accept, accept with conditions, or reject** the work; it does not depend on approving payment, the award’s value, or its outcome (Framework 12.5).

| Award type | How fees are calculated |
|---|---|
| **RFP — directed or open call** | A fixed fee for each milestone. |
| **Bounty** | One fee, equal to the applicable milestone fee. |

**What the fees cover**

- **Preparation and administration:** Milestone fees include scoping support, publication, public Q&A, intake, compliance checks, evaluation logistics, and scoring. These activities earn no separate fee.
- **Recovery plans:** The ordinary milestone fee includes reassessing corrected work under a recovery plan. Neither role earns an extra fee (Framework 11.9).

**How fees are paid**

Fees are **set in USD and paid in A**, using the Reference Rate **when payment is proposed**, through the published Program Cost schedule (Framework 13.4 and 13.3a).

Fees continue to accrue during a suspension under Framework 13.4a. On resumption, use the Reference Rate when the resumed payment is proposed, retaining the accrual-cycle allocation and rules for escalation to block producers in **Framework 13.4a**.

### The no-award fee

Each role earns **one no-award fee per engagement**, equal to its milestone fee, in these situations:

| Situation | Reference |
|---|---|
| An RFP closes without an award. | — |
| An award is terminated before all milestones are completed. | Framework 11.10 and 11.10a |
| An award decision expires under **Framework 10.10**, including its cycle-close cut-off and any applicable clock extensions. | Framework 10.10 |
| A bounty expires unclaimed. | Framework 26a, rule 5 |

The fee **replaces any milestone fee for the same determination**; both cannot be paid. Fees for earlier determinations remain payable, but no fees are paid for milestones never determined. Role-specific amounts and payment triggers are in **2.2 and 3.2**.

## 1. Setting and reviewing rates

Rates are set by **MSIG #5** and may change only by **block producers through an MSIG Resolution at the Threshold**; changes apply only to new engagements. The Committee cannot negotiate rates or add premiums (Framework 12.6.5).

Rates are reviewed annually under **Framework 14.3**, using cycle reports to assess the work required.

## 2. RFP Program Manager

### 2.1 Manager responsibilities

Each engagement covers **one RFP or bounty, from publication to closing**; a Manager may handle several engagements at once.

| Stage | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Publication** | Help draft and publish the RFP on the Portal; run public Q&A with all questions and answers available to every proposer and **no private guidance**. |
| **Intake** | Receive submissions and check compliance. |
| **Evaluation** | Coordinate evaluation; write the scored assessment against published criteria if **no Reviewer is engaged or the only Reviewer is a Committee member** (Framework 22.3), earning the additional fee in 2.2. |
| **Objections** | Maintain the objection register during the delay window and fix the final count when it closes. |
| **Delivery** | Coordinate with the awardee, with check-ins **at least every two weeks** during active work. |
| **Milestones** | Determine whether milestones are met and approve payments with the records required by Framework 11.3; during payment suspensions, publish determinations when made and complete the records when payments resume (13.4a). |
| **Escalation** | **Recommend termination in writing** when delivered work or services fall materially below the RFP’s requirements (Framework 11.10a). |
| **Closing** | Complete the closing requirements in **Framework 25.1** and any required post-award review under **Framework 25.2**. |
| **Handover** | On reassignment, provide a written handover of approval history, correspondence, and outstanding items (Framework 12.3). |

### 2.2 Fees

| Item | Fee (USD) | When payable |
|---|---|---|
| **RFP — each milestone** | **350** | On the Manager’s determination: accept, accept with conditions, or reject. |
| **RFP — each milestone with scored assessment** | **600** | On each milestone determination when the Manager wrote the scored assessment under Framework 22.3. Includes the **USD 250 assessment addition**. |
| **Bounty — one-time** | **350** | On the Manager’s written acceptance determination (Framework 26a). |
| **No-award fee** | **350**, or **600** if the Manager wrote the scored assessment | When the Committee records an event listed under *The no-award fee* above. |
| **Reassignment to a live RFP** | **500** | On assignment, for the incoming Manager’s preparation; the outgoing Manager keeps earned milestone fees and earns no further fees for that engagement. |
| **Post-award review** | **375** | On publication of a review required under **Framework 25.2**. |

Whether a Reviewer is needed is decided at scoping based on whether the work is technical (Framework 20.2), fixed at publication, and not changed later to reduce fees.

### 2.3 Workload

The Committee must publish each Manager’s workload in the cycle report (Framework 12.4). **Four concurrent engagements is a planning estimate, not a limit.**

## 3. Technical Reviewer

### 3.1 Reviewer responsibilities

Each engagement covers **one RFP or bounty** whose published statement says a Reviewer is engaged (Framework 20.2); that setting applies throughout the award.

| Stage | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Evaluation** | Submit an independent written score with reasons against the published criteria, without seeing other Reviewers’ scores first; any later discussion to resolve large differences must be recorded (Framework 22.3), subject to the Committee-member restrictions in 12.6. |
| **Milestones** | Provide a written assessment at **every milestone**: accept, accept with conditions, or reject with stated fixes. |
| **Escalation** | Recommend termination **in writing directly to the Committee** when delivered work or services fall materially below the required standard (Framework 11.10a). |

Reviewers also assess ongoing services, even when there is no separate deliverable.

### 3.2 Fees

| Item | Fee (USD) | When payable |
|---|---|---|
| **RFP — each milestone** | **450** | On the written assessment, whatever its conclusion. |
| **Bounty — one-time** | **450** | On the written assessment of the delivery. |
| **No-award fee** | **450** | Under *The no-award fee* above, whether or not the Reviewer filed the termination recommendation. |
| **Substitute Reviewer during an award** | **450 per remaining milestone, plus 375** | Milestone assessments at the normal rate, plus **375** for reading earlier assessments (Framework 12.6.1(3)). |

### 3.3 Committee members serving as Reviewers

Committee members receive the rates in **section 3.2** and must follow **Framework 12.6**, including its disclosure, recusal, and separate-payment requirements under **6.3c and 13.3a**.

## 4. Requirements for every engagement

| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Contract** | Sign the standard **VS LLC Independent Contractor Agreement** with the role’s **Schedule A in Exhibit F** before performing the role. |
| **Disclosure** | File the standard questionnaire **on-chain** under Framework 6.3a before being recorded as Manager of record or assigned as Reviewer; overdue annual refiling bars new assignments until filed. |
| **Conflicts** | Follow the Framework’s conflict rules and ban register; Committee members must also meet the one-member-per-affiliation rule. |
| **Publication** | Report every engagement and fee in the cycle report; for member-Reviewers, include the member, RFP, cumulative cycle fees, and engagement count against the cap (Framework 13.5). |

## Appendix — Example cycle

Six RFPs are published, four with Reviewers and two using the Manager assessment variant without Reviewers. One Reviewer-engaged RFP closes without an award; the other five have four milestones each, and three require post-award reviews.

| Item | USD |
|---|---|
| Manager — 3 standard awards × 4 milestones × 350, plus 2 assessment-variant awards × 4 milestones × 600 | 9,000 |
| Manager — no-award fee for the sixth RFP | 350 |
| Manager — 3 post-award reviews × 375 | 1,125 |
| Reviewer — 3 awards × 4 milestones × 450, plus one no-award fee of 450 | 5,850 |
| **Total Manager and Reviewer fees** | **16,325** |
| Cycle cap for these fees, Portal, and administration (MSIG #5 Part D) | 40,000 |
| **Remaining for Portal, hosting, and administration** | **23,675** |

This example does not price Portal, hosting, or administration; those costs must also fit within the **40,000** cap.
