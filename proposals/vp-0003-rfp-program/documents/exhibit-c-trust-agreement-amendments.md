## What this exhibit is for

Exhibit C is the letter under which counsel's conforming amendments to the Trust Agreement were once attached to MSIG #5. Those amendments bring the Trust Agreement into line with the restructured program: the Oversight Committee continued as the five-seat Network Steering Committee, its standing mandate over the Program Account, and the roles of the Trustee and VS LLC under the Framework.

## Why it is not attached

MSIG #5 states that **there is no Exhibit C**. The amendments are administered off-chain by the VST and VS LLC, and Part J of the resolution directs the Trustee to execute them **without attaching their text**. They are not part of what block producers approve by executing MSIG #5.

## Why the letter is kept

The remaining letters are unchanged. Exhibits D, E, and F are cited by letter throughout the Framework, the Schedule A templates, and the platform requirements, and renaming them to close the gap would silently redirect every one of those citations. This placeholder exists so that a reader who meets the gap between Exhibit B and Exhibit D finds the reason here rather than assuming a document is missing.
