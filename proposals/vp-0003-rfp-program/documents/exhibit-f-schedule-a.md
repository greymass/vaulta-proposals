## How to use these schedules

**VS LLC** engages Committee members, Program Managers, Technical Reviewers, and awardees under its **standard Independent Contractor Agreement**; use the Schedule A for the role.

## Common terms

**These Common terms form part of each Schedule A below and must accompany the signed schedule.** Role-specific requirements apply only to the roles named.

**Framework** means the **Vaulta Network RFP Framework (Exhibit A to MSIG #5)**.

The main agreement applies to every schedule:

| Provision | Effect |
|---|---|
| **§2** | The contract does **not appoint anyone to a governance role**; Committee seats require MSIG appointments. |
| **§5** | Services tied to an MSIG-appointed role end automatically when MSIG removes the person. |
| **§6** | The contract gives no authority to bind the Company, Trust, or Network; Committee signing authority comes from MSIG and the on-chain permission. |
| **§7** | No compensation may be a distribution, profit participation, private benefit, or token-based incentive. |
| **§8** | Confidentiality survives for **2 years**, including proposal contents and evaluation deliberations. |
| **§9** | Work Product vests in the **VST**, regardless of funding source; Awardee section 7 defines Work Product by award shape and does not disapply §9. |
| **§11** | Return or securely delete credentials on termination and cooperate with rotation and revocation. |
| **§13** | Contractor indemnifies, without removing indemnification available under the Trust Agreement or an MSIG-approved agreement for authorized governance roles. |
| **§15** | VS LLC holds legal names for tax and banking; public handles are permitted, and contractors must not disclose each other’s legal names. |

**Conflicts.** Committee members, Managers, and Reviewers must follow the Framework’s conflict and recusal rules. For Managers and Reviewers, **Framework 6.4 applies 6.1 and 6.2**, including interests disclosed in questionnaire Parts **3, 5, and 7**; role-specific Treasury and bidding restrictions remain in each schedule.

**Disclosure and annual filing:**

| Applies to | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Committee members, Managers, and Reviewers** | File the standard **on-chain disclosure questionnaire** under Framework 6.3a; update within **10 business days** of a change and refile annually. It is the conflict declaration, not an additional document. |
| **Managers and Reviewers** | Filing must precede recording as Manager of record or assignment as Reviewer; overdue annual refiling **bars new assignments until filed**. |
| **Committee members** | File within the **30-day period in Committee Schedule A, clause 7**; annual refiling more than **30 days overdue suspends pay until filed**. A member’s Reviewer engagement also requires the advance update in Reviewer Schedule A, clause 6. |

**Material conflict breaches.** Committee members, Managers, and Reviewers acknowledge that a material breach carries a **permanent ban from every program role and payment**, confirmed and lifted only at **15/21** (Framework 6.6). Examples include self-dealing, undisclosed interests, payment from a proposer, misuse of proposal information, confidentiality breaches, and signing while recused. Minor or procedural lapses are corrected and recorded, not banned. The Awardee’s acknowledgment and ban-register warranty are in its clause 6.

**Public identity.** Use **one stable public identity** for the term so votes, recusals, and approvals remain attributable; anonymous or changing identities do not satisfy this requirement.

## Schedule A — Steering Committee Member

**1. Role:** Vaulta Network Steering Committee Member — [Core Development / Marketing / Business Development / General (At-Large) / Community] seat.

**The Common terms above are incorporated into this Schedule A and must accompany it when signed.**

**2. Work:**

- Attend meetings **at least monthly**, and **at least twice monthly during an open RFP cycle**.
- Lead needs triage, scoping, and category diligence for the seat’s portfolio.
- Evaluate proposals against published criteria and vote on publication, awards, withholding, and termination.
- Hold a personal key on `rfp.vst`’s Committee permission and sign approved disbursements.
- Follow the **Vaulta Network RFP Framework**, including conflicts, recusals, publication, and reporting.
- Contribute to cycle reports, the annual review, and Committee self-review.

**3. Pay:**

- **USD [2,500] monthly**, invoiced and paid **monthly in arrears in A** at the Reference Rate on the payment date: use the conversion rules in **Framework 13.4 and Exhibit D, Part 6**, including the **24-hour staleness check and CoinMarketCap fallback**.
- Pay starts on the **later of contract signature and receipt of funds in the Program Account**.
- During a rate-source payment suspension under Framework 13.4a, entitlement **accrues** and is paid on resumption at the rate then current.

**4. Expenses:** Reasonable, documented, **pre-approved** travel to Network events and role-specific tooling.

**5. Records and reporting:** Record attendance and votes in published minutes; follow the **Common terms’ disclosure and annual-filing requirements**.

**6. Limits:**

- Contractor holds **one of five signing units** on `rfp.vst`; moving funds requires **four signatures**, with no unilateral account authority.
- Keys must not be shared, delegated, held by an employer or affiliate, or used to sign for another member, including a recused member.
- Contractor **cannot approve milestones or serve as Manager of record on any RFP**, without exception (Framework 12.2a).
- Technical Reviewer work requires a **separate engagement and signed Technical Reviewer Schedule A**, subject to **Framework 12.6 and all conditions in that schedule’s clause 6**.
- Contractor must not bid on **any Vaulta RFP during the term or for 6 months afterwards**, directly or through an entity in which they hold a material interest.
- Contractor must not be Treasury-affiliated; disclose any change within **10 business days**, with signing weight **suspended immediately** and referral to MSIG.

**7. Additional terms:**

- For a material-conflict allegation under Framework 6.6, provide Contractor the allegation in writing and **10 business days to respond before MSIG votes**; the Common terms’ ban acknowledgment applies.
- **Suspend pay automatically** on referral to MSIG for removal or three consecutive unexcused missed meetings, pending resolution; a material-conflict referral immediately suspends **role, signing weight, and pay**.
- Signing weight and pay begin only after the contract is signed, the questionnaire is filed on-chain, and a signing key is registered; the **30-day deadline** runs from the **later of appointment execution and VS LLC publishing that the disclosure register is open**.
- Surrender key weight on removal or replacement; retain it during authorized month-to-month continuation.
- Report key loss or suspected compromise **immediately to the Chair and block producers**, and stop signing until replacement.

## Schedule A — RFP Program Manager

**1. Role:** RFP Program Manager; Contractor **must not be a seated Committee member** (Framework 12.2a).

**The Common terms above are incorporated into this Schedule A and must accompany it when signed.**

**2. Work:** For each RFP or bounty managed:

- Support drafting and publication; run public Q&A with **all questions and answers shared with every proposer and no private guidance**.
- Receive submissions, check compliance, and coordinate evaluation.
- Write the **scored assessment** against published criteria when the RFP says **no Reviewer is engaged or the only Reviewer is a Committee member**; consider that member’s written note without being bound by it. **Exhibit B prices this assessment variant.**
- Maintain the objection register through the delay window and fix the count at its close.
- Coordinate with awardees, including check-ins **at least every two weeks** during active work.
- Determine milestones and approve payment, recording the milestone, criteria, determination and reasons, Reviewer assessment if any, amount, and **verified receiving account**.
- **Recommend termination in writing when required by Framework 11.10a**, including for materially inadequate work or service and persistent failure to meet published service levels.
- For awards meeting the review threshold in MSIG #5 Part D, measured on **total contract value**, publish a short post-award review **within 20 business days** of final acceptance, publication of termination, or the bounty acceptance determination; cover whether the need was met, the budget was right, and the Network would repeat the award. Give the awardee **5 business days before publication to respond**, and publish the review and any response on the Portal; **the award cannot close before publication** (Framework 25.2). Fees follow Exhibit B.
- Provide a written handover of approval history and open items on reassignment.

**3. Pay:** **Exhibit B’s rates and payment rules form part of this Schedule A, including its Common payment rules and sections 1 and 2.2**, including payment triggers, no-award fees, evaluation work, recovery-plan reassessment, reassignment, post-award reviews, and accrual during rate-source suspensions.

Fees are **fixed, not hourly or a percentage of an award**, and do not depend on milestone approval. They are denominated in **USD and paid in A at the Reference Rate on the payment date**, under Framework 13.4 and the calculation rules in Exhibit D, Part 6.

**4. Expenses:** Pre-approved and documented only.

**5. Records and reporting:** Report status, milestone approval records, qualifying post-award reviews, and concurrent RFP workload for cycle reports; follow the **Common terms’ disclosure and annual-filing requirements**.

**6. Limits:**

- Contractor cannot be a seated Committee member; **no availability exception or waiver applies**. Committee appointment **automatically ends this engagement for every RFP**, which must be reassigned within **5 business days** (Framework 12.2a).
- Contractor has **no award vote or signing key**; milestone approval alone does not authorize payment, which requires **four Committee signatures and the on-chain delay**.
- Contractor must not approve or assess conflicted milestones and must notify the Committee for reassignment; the **Treasury affiliation bar in Framework 2.4a applies separately and absolutely**.
- Contractor must not bid on RFPs they work on **during the engagement or for 6 months afterwards**, or be Treasury-affiliated.
- Refer approval to the Committee if the oracle is more than **24 hours stale and the CoinMarketCap fallback is unavailable**, or the rate differs by more than **15% from the previous payment under that award**; Contractor cannot approve alone.
- A termination recommendation **does not terminate the award**: Contractor must not stop work, withhold delivered Work Product, suspend services, refuse a data export, or tell an awardee the engagement has ended. Only the Committee terminates, at **two-thirds, minimum 3**; the Committee cancels pending payments at **3 of 5 within 2 business days**, not automatically on filing.
- Contractor must not file a termination recommendation if conflicted or no longer engaged on the RFP; notify the Committee instead. The Committee may **strike an improper recommendation by simple majority**.
- A **good-faith termination recommendation must not be grounds for ending or declining to renew this engagement**; the fee is unaffected by whether the Committee acts.

## Schedule A — Technical Reviewer

**1. Role:** Technical Reviewer — [RFP reference].

**The Common terms above are incorporated into this Schedule A and must accompany it when signed.**

**2. Work:** The Committee decides at scoping whether the work is technical and needs a Reviewer, including for **Service awards**; the published Reviewer-engaged setting applies throughout the award (Framework 20.2).

- Independently score compliant proposals in writing against published criteria **without seeing other Reviewers’ scores**; reconcile large differences only after submission and on the record.
- **Committee-member exception:** Submit a written note instead of scores and take **no part in score reconciliation**; if Contractor is the only Reviewer, the Manager writes the scored assessment (Framework 12.6).
- Give a **written assessment at every milestone** where the published Reviewer-engaged setting is “yes”: accept, accept with conditions, or reject with specified fixes.
- File required termination recommendations **directly with the Committee**, independently of the Manager, for materially inadequate work or service, including persistent failure to meet published service levels (Framework 11.10a); the Manager must not withhold them, and **both positions must be filed and published if they disagree**.

**3. Pay:** **Exhibit B’s rates and payment rules form part of this Schedule A, including its Common payment rules and sections 1 and 3.2**, including payment triggers, no-award fees, evaluation work, recovery-plan reassessment, substitute-Reviewer fees, and accrual during rate-source suspensions.

Fees are **fixed, not hourly or a percentage of an award**, and do not depend on the assessment’s conclusion. They are denominated in **USD and paid in A at the Reference Rate on the payment date**, under Framework 13.4 and the calculation rules in Exhibit D, Part 6.

**4. Expenses:** Pre-approved and documented only.

**5. Records and reporting:** Provide written scores with reasons, subject to the Committee-member exception, and written milestone assessments; follow the **Common terms’ disclosure and annual-filing requirements**.

**6. Limits:**

The Reviewer role gives **no award vote, signing key, or authority to approve milestone payments**; the Manager approves. A Reviewer who also holds a Committee seat retains that seat’s authority, subject to every condition below (Framework 12.6).

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | MSIG must first set the concurrent-engagement cap; the engagement must stay within it. There is **no cap on total Reviewer fees**. The Committee must publish a finding that **no suitable unconflicted external Reviewer is available**; Contractor recuses from that finding and the engagement vote. |
| **Full Committee** | All **five seats must be filled**; a vacancy suspends the Reviewer engagement. If Contractor is the only Reviewer, engage a substitute; without one, the Manager writes the scored assessment before award, and a live award’s milestone goes to MSIG. If a substitute is engaged, resumption after the vacancy is filled requires a Committee decision; fees are prorated to work performed. |
| **One member per RFP** | No more than **one Committee member** may review the same RFP. |
| **Evaluation** | Contractor must not draft acceptance criteria or the milestone schedule, score proposals, or reconcile scores; submit a written note instead. If Contractor is the only Reviewer, the **Manager writes the scored assessment** (Framework 22.3). |
| **Award decisions** | Contractor retains the seat’s **award vote and signing key**, including signing milestone payments; the Reviewer role gives **no authority to approve milestones** and never permits serving as Manager of record (Framework 12.2a). |
| **Termination votes** | Contractor must recuse from **every termination recommendation, recovery plan, and strike decision** on an award they reviewed, whoever filed it, including after the Reviewer engagement ends or is suspended. |
| **Own fees** | Recuse from the fee’s schedule line and signing its payment; pay **each member-Reviewer separately**, never combined with another member’s fee. Use **Exhibit B rates**, which the Committee cannot vary; Reviewer fees are **Program Costs, not Committee pay**. |
| **Disclosure and reporting** | File a questionnaire update **before the engagement begins**, without the usual 10-business-day window (Framework 6.3c). Report each engagement and fee **by member and RFP**, cumulative cycle fees, and engagement count against the cap. |

- A termination recommendation **does not terminate the award**: Contractor must not stop work, withhold delivered Work Product, suspend services, refuse a data export, or tell an awardee the engagement has ended. Only the Committee terminates, at **two-thirds, minimum 3**; the Committee cancels pending payments at **3 of 5 within 2 business days**, not automatically on filing.
- Contractor must not file a termination recommendation if conflicted or no longer engaged on the RFP; notify the Committee instead. The Committee may **strike an improper recommendation by simple majority**.
- A **good-faith termination recommendation must not be grounds for ending or declining to renew this engagement**; the fee is unaffected by whether the Committee acts.
- Contractor must not bid on an RFP they review **during the engagement or for 6 months afterwards**, or be Treasury-affiliated.

**6. Limits and award terms:**

**Disbursement condition.** This Agreement depends on the corresponding on-chain disbursement clearing its delay; cancellation by block producers or by the Committee following objections **terminates the Agreement without liability on either side**.

**Clawback and other funding.** Contractor must repay amounts for milestones later found undelivered or misrepresented; this is a contractual remedy, because on-chain transfers cannot be reversed. Disclose any **other Network funding for the same work**.

**Bans.** Contractor warrants that neither Contractor nor anyone named in the proposal is on the **published ban register**, and acknowledges the **permanent ban from future awards** for a material conflict breach, confirmed and lifted only at **15/21** (Framework 6.6).

**Termination recommendations (Framework 11.10a).** A Manager or engaged Reviewer may recommend termination for materially inadequate work or service, including persistent service-level failures:

- Cancel pending payment proposals and approve **no further milestones for payment** until the Committee decides.
- Give Contractor the written recommendation and **10 business days to respond**; publish the response with it.
- The Committee must decide **within 15 business days after the response window closes** and publish reasons, including a decision not to terminate; it may direct a recovery plan and reassessment date under Framework 11.9.
- If no outcome reaches its threshold in time, the recommendation is **deemed declined** and the freeze lifts; a cancelled payment may be re-proposed **without another award vote**, with a **fresh delay**.
- Extend any **60-day contracting period** under Framework 10.10 by the freeze’s duration. **Only the Committee terminates.**

**Termination consequences.** Section 5 of the Agreement governs termination for non-delivery or cause; accepted milestones are paid, unearned amounts are not proposed, and delivered Work Product remains with the VST under §9. **Service handover materials and the data export become due on termination; the Embedded licence back in 7.3a survives.**

**Rate-source failure (Framework 13.4a).** If the **`eosusd` pair or `delphioracle` contract** is unavailable, renamed, or deprecated, use the CoinMarketCap fallback; **suspend payment only if that is also unavailable**, until it returns or MSIG designates a replacement source:

- Suspension is **not a breach by either party**. Continue recording milestone determinations; approve payment on resumption at the rate then current.
- Extend delivery dates and any **60-day contracting period** by the suspension’s length.
- After **more than 30 business days** of suspension, Contractor may terminate on **10 business days’ written notice**; the notice **lapses if payments resume before it expires**.
- On Contractor’s termination, pay accepted milestones on resumption, do not propose unearned amounts, require the section 7 handover, and have the Manager record and publish the termination (Framework 11.8).
- The **long-stop clock does not run while a Framework 11.10a termination recommendation is open** on the award.
- The disbursement condition remains unchanged: **a payment never proposed has not been cancelled**.

**Price risk.** The award’s **USD value is fixed**; Contractor bears no A-price exposure between award and payment approval. The **A quantity is fixed at approval**, so Contractor bears price changes during the **168-hour delay, or 72 hours if urgent**, and any additional delay after cancellation and re-proposal.

## Open points for counsel

1. Confirm that an **entity awardee’s §9 assignment** covers its employees’ and subcontractors’ work.
2. Confirm that the **disbursement condition** operates alongside §18 (entire agreement) and §19 (amendments).
3. Confirm that **Committee signing under MSIG and the on-chain permission** is compatible with §6’s limit on expenditure authority and §2.
4. Confirm that a **stable public handle** satisfies Framework attribution requirements for votes, recusals, and milestone approvals under §15.
5. Confirm the **rate-source suspension and termination terms** against the main agreement: neither party in breach, extended delivery dates, **more than 30 business days** before **10 business days’ notice**, and notice lapsing if payments resume; suspension applies only when the fallback is also unavailable.
6. Review the **Trustee Compensation and Indemnification Acknowledgment** and resolve available governance-role indemnification **before MSIG #5 Part H is final**; it remains unreviewed.
7. Confirm that **§5’s general for-cause and without-cause terms apply to awardees**; its automatic-termination provision addresses MSIG-appointed roles.
8. Confirm that **§9 permits the 7.2 Pre-Existing IP carve-out and 7.3a licence back through Schedule A**; the blocking restrictions remain in Awardee section 7. Defining Work Product by award shape does not itself narrow §9.
